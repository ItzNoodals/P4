What our Website offers

Our website is meant for people to get into the game of baseball and to learn how it came to be. We want to show how the past made what baseball is today. We do this by showing all the past teams that were created and how they transitioned into the teams we have now.

Past and current standings to show how the league was split up amoungst each other and how the number of teams grew. Historical moments and notable players that shaped baseball. Current rules of baseball to have people understand how the game is palyed.

Baseball is America's past time and we want to show why that statement is what it is today.
